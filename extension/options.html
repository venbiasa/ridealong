ridealong

Lets a local tool read a browser-gated service through your own signed-in session. To pair, start the bridge (ridealong serve): it opens a page here and you click Pair.

Allowed hosts

A host is added when you click Allow on the page that opens the first time a request goes to it.

    Advanced

    Only if you started the bridge with --port. It always listens on 127.0.0.1.
